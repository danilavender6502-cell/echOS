The Hundred Eyes

echOS Cycle 25 — Argus Panoptes, and the peacock's tail. The head watches the sea; the sea faces the Reader; the Reader looks back.

Ten watches, one per ten eyes. Before each watch opens, call the stare — will its eyes be open, or shut? — then speak one word for the watch. The seal of the word is the sum of its letters, a=1 to z=26. An odd seal opens the watch's eyes; an even seal shuts them. The calling is judged and recorded. The record is kept and left alone — it is never interpreted.
