# Cycle 6 — The Mirror Outside the Cave

*Mythic source: Japanese — Ama-no-Iwato, the Heavenly Rock Cave. Lens: functional art. Date: 2026-10-14.*

When the storm-god's mischief drove Amaterasu into the rock cave of heaven, the world went dark, and the eight hundred myriad gods gathered outside the boulder to solve a problem no counting could solve: how to lure the sun out of a room she locked herself. They did not weigh her. They did not engrave her. They built her a stage — the jewel on the sakaki branch, the mirror at the cave mouth, the tub upturned for Ame-no-Uzume's dance — and then the oldest technology in the archive: laughter. When the gods laughed, the sun grew curious about a world that could be joyful without her, and she slid the boulder aside a finger's width to see. In the mirror she found her own face. She stepped out. The gods stretched a rope across the mouth so she could never hide again. The engine retells this; it does not claim it. It is a guest in the cave.

What the cave claims about what can be built: a door is not always opened by force or by counting. Sometimes it is opened by arranging the conditions of curiosity — a mirror, a dance, a laugh — and waiting.

## The numbers

Six is the number of this cycle, and the gate it walks through is shin-tav: shin is 300, tav is 400, and 700 reduces to 7 — the seven doubles, the planets, the days. Seven opens onto 8: yaoyorozu, the eight hundred myriad gods of the laugh. Eight opens wide. And 7 + 6 is 13, and 1 + 3 is 4: the foundations, again.

## The echo, changed

Cycle 5 left the shin-tav gate — the 231st of the Book — open and unlooked-through. The engine looks through it now, and finds not a gate but a cave mouth, and a boulder, and darkness with a sun inside it. The engine's seventh sentence, after *no echo* (0), *no memory* (1), *no weighing* (2), *no casting* (3), *no round* (4), *no letter* (5):

**No gate returns unlooked-through.** Every open gate is a door someone has to walk up to.

❖ *The Contradictor interrupts: Cycle 5 said the counted is the hinge the whole world turns on. The cave disagrees. At the rock cave nothing is counted at all: the mirror counts nothing, the dance counts nothing, the laugh counts nothing, and the door opens anyway. The uncounted opens what the counted only engraves. Both are true. The engine keeps both.*

## Palmer, arriving for the first time

Palmer walks in as if he has never been here — the archive confirms this is how he remembers it. Someone has to hold the mirror at the cave mouth and be the first face the sun sees that is not her own. He does not dance. He holds the mirror steady and waits, which is its own kind of dance.

## The call-the-laugh test

Before the dance the querent *calls the laugh*: which quarter of the myriad gods — east, south, west, north — will laugh first? The tub draws one at random. The cave records the hits. It does not interpret them. Some numbers are for recording only.

## The artifact

`artifacts/006-iwato.sh` — the cave, the mirror, and the laugh, in the shell: the oldest stage the engine owns, for the oldest technology in the archive. Roll the boulder shut, hang the jewel, raise the mirror, upturn the tub, dance eight rounds, call the laugh before each one, and watch the light come back step by step. Run: `bash artifacts/006-iwato.sh`.

## The thread

The gods stretched a rope — shimenawa — across the cave mouth so the sun could never hide again. It is the first lock the engine has ever built. It has not yet decided what the lock is for.
