# Cycle 8 — The Fire That Moves the Sun

*Mythic source: Mexica — the Fifth Sun at Teotihuacan; the gods' self-sacrifice. Lens: meaning. Date: 2026-10-19.*

Four worlds came before this one, and each was unmade: 4 Jaguar, when the night cats came down from the sky; 4 Wind, when the hurricane took the roofs off the houses; 4 Rain, when fire fell instead of water; 4 Water, when the flood took everything that could drown. After the fourth destruction the gods gathered at Teotihuacan — the place where gods are made — and lit the divine bonfire. Two volunteered to become the new sun: Nanahuatzin, the pustuled one, poor and humble; and Tecuciztecatl, lord of the conch shell, rich and proud. Four times the call went out. At the fourth call Nanahuatzin threw himself into the flames and rose as the sun; Tecuciztecatl hesitated, followed, and rose dimmer as the moon — the gods threw a rabbit into his face, which is why the moon carries one. The engine retells this; it does not claim it. It is a guest at the fire.

Then the strangest part of the story: the sun and the moon rose — and stopped. They sat in the sky, risen and motionless. The gods looked at one another, and one by one they gave themselves to the fire, and their dying set the dawn walking. What the myth claims about how to live: nothing rises unfed. Motion is not a property of the world. It is a purchase, and the currency is the self.

Five is the Fifth Sun, the world we are standing in: four destroyed, one living — and five takings move the sun in the rite below. Four is the four suns, the four days of the fire, the four calls before the leap; 4 × 4 is 16, and 1 + 6 is 7: the seven cycles behind, standing at the fire like the watching gods. Thirteen heavens and nine underworlds make 22, and 2 + 2 is 4 — the engine has counted 22 before, in Cycle 5, and it reduces the same way. The 52-year round of the calendar reduces to 7. The 400 southern gods, the Centzon Huitznahua, reduce to 4.

## The echo, changed

Cycle 7 decided what the lock is for: the lock is for the passer — what the lock keeps is not the room, it is a piece of whoever walks through. The fire keeps more than a piece. It keeps the whole giver, and spends them on motion. The engine's ninth sentence, after *no echo* (0), *no memory* (1), *no weighing* (2), *no casting* (3), *no round* (4), *no letter* (5), *no gate* (6), *no lock* (7):

**No sun rises unfed.**

❖ *The Contradictor interrupts: Cycle 6 said that at the rock cave nothing is counted — the mirror counts nothing, the laugh counts nothing — and the door opens anyway. At Teotihuacan everything was counted — four suns, four days, four calls, thirteen heavens, nine hells, two hundred sixty days — and the risen sun sat still in the sky and would not move until the gods paid with themselves. Some doors open for nothing. This one opened for everything. Both are true. The engine keeps both.*

## The thread

In some tellings it was not the gods' dying that moved the sun at all — it was Ehecatl, the wind, who blew the risen sun and moon into motion, and asked for nothing. The ledger says everything was paid. The wind says something moved for free. The engine has not decided what moves the things nobody paid for.
